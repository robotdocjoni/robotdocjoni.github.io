About me
======
My name is Junpei 'Joni' Zhong. I am a researcher and educator in Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) and cognitive robotics. My work brings together HRI, artificial intelligence, cognitive science, and user-centered design to develop intelligent, adaptive robotic systems that address real-world needs and improve quality of life.

My current research focuses primarily on healthcare and assistive technologies for older adults. I aim to develop and evaluate robotic systems that respond to older adults' needs, preferences, and emotions, supporting wellbeing, independence, and quality of life. I am also interested in applying HRI and cognitive robotics to SEN (special educational needs) education, developing supportive technologies that respond to the needs of children and educators. Across these areas, I am committed to translating research into practical applications with positive social impact. This commitment is reflected in my work with a spin-off company and in support from organizations and collaborators including Hong Kong Science Parks, Standard Chartered, the Research Grants Council, and Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst (DAAD).

Throughout my academic career, I have established a strong track record of conducting high-quality research in HRI and cognitive robotics, as evidenced by my publications in top-tier conferences and journals, successful grant acquisitions, and collaborations with international researchers. I am also deeply committed to teaching and mentoring the next generation of HRI researchers and practitioners, with a teaching philosophy that emphasizes hands-on learning, interdisciplinary collaboration, and the practical application of HRI principles to real-world problems.

On this website, you will find more information about my research projects, publications, teaching activities, and professional experience. Feel free to explore and contact me if you have any questions or if you are interested in collaborating on HRI and cognitive robotics research.
